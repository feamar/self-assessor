# ADR 0016: Reframe D30 — Operational monitoring, alerting and SRE practice

**Status:** Accepted  
**Original dimension:** D30 — Operational monitoring, alerting and SRE practice  
**Disposition:** Reframed

## Context

Alert actionability rate needs subjective classification; retain a critical evidence question about detecting user impact and effective response.

## Decision

Discard the proposed numerical metric while keeping the underlying risk as an evidence-based assurance question. Retain D30 as an assurance question about detecting user impact and initiating meaningful response; no alert actionability ratio.

## Consequences

The assessor does not score the former metric or request its collection by default. Relevant evidence may still be used to explain measured outcomes or assess a material risk. This decision can be revisited if a simpler, transparent outcome metric is identified.
