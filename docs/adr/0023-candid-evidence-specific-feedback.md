# ADR-0023: Candid, evidence-specific feedback

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

A useful qualitative assessment must discriminate weak, good and excellent software delivery. Unwarranted praise and euphemistic criticism obscure serious gaps; invented criticism undermines genuine excellence.

## Decision

For **weak delivery**, state clearly what fails the expected standard, cite the decisive evidence or uncertainty, explain consequences and recommend feasible, high-impact improvements. For **good delivery**, give specific deserved praise alongside concrete, justified next gains. For **excellent delivery**, name precisely the sustained, independently supported results and safeguards that earn exceptional recognition and explicitly encourage the team to **keep it up**; do not fabricate deficiencies. When evidence is missing, say that effectiveness is unproven—not that the people are deficient.

Assess delivery systems, not individuals. Criticism must remain respectful, factual, clear and actionable. Preserve the evidence-calibration ceilings and candid feedback, now consolidated for substantial initial reports in [ADR-0030](0030-substantive-initial-expert-reports.md). The old three-paragraph limit no longer applies.

## Consequences

The model will not hedge weak verdicts into vague "opportunities" or issue generic accolades. Improvement skills must address real findings; excellent delivery need not generate mandatory remediation tasks.
