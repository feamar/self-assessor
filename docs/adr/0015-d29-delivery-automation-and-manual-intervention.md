# ADR 0015: Retire D29 — Delivery automation and manual intervention

**Status:** Accepted  
**Original dimension:** D29 — Delivery automation and manual intervention  
**Disposition:** Retired

## Context

Percentage of automated steps measures implementation method; lead time, frequency and recovery measure its intended effect.

## Decision

Remove this as a standalone assessed dimension. Observe lead time, deployment frequency and reliability; ask about manual bottlenecks only when needed to explain them.

## Consequences

The assessor does not score the former metric or request its collection by default. Relevant evidence may still be used to explain measured outcomes or assess a material risk. This decision can be revisited if a simpler, transparent outcome metric is identified.
