# ADR 0009: Reframe D19 — Dependencies and supply chain

**Status:** Accepted  
**Original dimension:** D19 — Dependencies and supply chain  
**Disposition:** Reframed

## Context

Vulnerability exposure time has ambiguous start/end events and specialist applicability decisions; retain checks for concrete supply-chain risk.

## Decision

Discard the proposed numerical metric while keeping the underlying risk as an evidence-based assurance question. Retain D19 as an assurance check for dependency provenance, known high-impact vulnerabilities and risk handling.

## Consequences

The assessor does not score the former metric or request its collection by default. Relevant evidence may still be used to explain measured outcomes or assess a material risk. This decision can be revisited if a simpler, transparent outcome metric is identified.
